Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026 · Last updated: September 14, 2026
Controller: Hermes Publish (Billion&Co) · Contact: support@hermes-publish.app

1. Overview

Hermes Publish ("we", "us") is a web app that lets you publish videos you own to your own TikTok account via TikTok's official API (open.tiktokapis.com). This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can delete it. We comply with TikTok's Developer Policies and applicable data-protection laws.

2. Data we collect

  • TikTok OAuth data (only after you authorize): open_id, display name, avatar URL (scope user.info.basic), and the OAuth access_token/refresh_token + expiry. We do not request email, phone, DMs, or follower lists.
  • Content you choose to upload: video file, caption, cover image, privacy setting (public/friends/private) — sent to /v2/video/publish on your behalf.
  • Technical logs: publish status (publish_id, upload result), timestamps, IP-derived country for rate-limiting. No advertising ID.

3. How we use it

  • Authenticate you and keep the session alive (refresh_token rotation).
  • Upload/publish videos per your explicit action. No auto-posting without your click/spreadsheet/queue trigger.
  • Show publish history and troubleshoot failures.

Legal bases: consent (OAuth) and legitimate interest (security, abuse prevention). We do not sell data and do not use it for advertising.

4. Storage & security

  • Tokens encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) on our server in Singapore (or your self-hosted M75). Access restricted to the app process.
  • Video files are streamed to TikTok and not retained after successful publish unless you enable "keep copy" (then auto-deleted after 30 days).
  • HTTPS everywhere; HSTS; token rotation on each refresh.

5. Sharing & disclosure

We share data only with TikTok APIs to perform the publish you requested. We do not share with third parties, analytics SDKs, or advertisers. We disclose only if required by law.

6. Retention

  • Tokens: until you revoke access (TikTok Settings → Apps → Hermes Publish → Revoke) or request deletion.
  • Publish logs: 90 days, then anonymized.
  • Backups: encrypted, 30-day retention.

7. Your rights

You may at any time: revoke TikTok access, request export of your data, or request deletion at support@hermes-publish.app. Deletion is completed within 7 days and confirmed by email. For self-hosted installs, delete ~/.hermes/secrets/tiktok.env locally.

8. Children's privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13 and not to TikTok users under TikTok's minimum age. We do not knowingly collect children's data.

9. International transfers

If you are in the EEA/UK/Indonesia, data may be processed in Singapore on TikTok's infrastructure. We rely on Standard Contractual Clauses where required.

10. Cookies

This legal site uses no tracking cookies. The web app uses a single httpOnly session cookie for CSRF/OAuth state. No third-party pixels.

11. Changes

We will post material changes here and update the effective date. Continued use after notice constitutes acceptance.
