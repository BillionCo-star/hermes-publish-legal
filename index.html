Hermes Publish connects your TikTok account via the official TikTok API to upload scheduled videos you own. We only request user.info.basic and video.upload — nothing else. No inbox, no comments, no deletion.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

What we do

Schedule and publish videos you created to your own TikTok account via open.tiktokapis.com/v2/video/publish. You authorize once via TikTok OAuth; we store an encrypted access_token.

What we don't do

We do not read DMs, followers list beyond basic profile, or post to other accounts. No auto-commenting, scraping, or follower manipulation.

Data & deletion

Tokens expire and auto-refresh. Revoke anytime in TikTok Settings → Apps. Request data deletion via support@hermes-publish.app (processed within 7 days).
